About International News Hub

One country's news at a time, as its own press reports it, readable in your language.

How it works

Every hour we read the public news feeds that major outlets in each country publish for syndication. For each item, an AI model writes a one- or two-sentence summary in its own words from the outlet's headline and feed description, and translates the headline. We show the summary, the outlet's name, and a link to the original article. We do not reproduce article text, and we do not host the articles.

Accuracy

Summaries are machine-written and can be wrong or miss context. They state only what the outlet's headline and description say. For anything that matters, read the original at the link. Nothing here is our reporting.

Countries and languages

Twenty countries to start, four to six outlets each, chosen for size and reach. Twelve display languages. More of both as we go.

For publishers

We link to you and send readers your way. If you would rather not be included, or want a summary corrected or removed, email asmith3796@gmail.com with the link and we will act on it promptly.

Who

An independent project, built and run from New Jersey. No ads, no tracking, no accounts.
